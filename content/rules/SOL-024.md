# SOL-024 · Stale / unchecked oracle price

A Pyth/Switchboard price with no staleness (publish-slot age) or confidence check → attacker trades/liquidates at a mispriced value. Fix: `get_price_no_older_than(...)`; reject wide-confidence.

**Enforcement — machine-checkable.** A deterministic pattern flags this automatically in the [CLI](../../cli), the [GitHub Action](../../action.yml), the [Semgrep ruleset](../../semgrep), the [VS Code extension](../../extensions/vscode), and the [MCP server](../../mcp).

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the oracle price read without a staleness (publish-slot age) or confidence check.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. get_price_no_older_than (staleness, with a max-age bound that reflects the protocol's real staleness tolerance — u64::MAX or an unreasonably large age is NOT sufficient) AND a confidence-interval bound are both enforced before use
2. the price feeds a non-economic / display-only path that cannot trigger a trade, liquidation, or any other economic action — neither on-chain NOR via an off-chain consumer (e.g. a keeper reading emitted price events to trigger liquidations, rebalancing, or collateral updates)
