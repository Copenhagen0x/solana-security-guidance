# SOL-026 · Duplicate mutable account (native programs)

Two accounts that must differ aren't checked → attacker passes one twice, collapsing a delta check. Fix: `require_keys_neq!`. *(Anchor rejects dupe-mutable `Account<>` (err 2040); NOT `AccountLoader`/`UncheckedAccount`/remaining_accounts.)*

**Enforcement — review-only.** A judgment-call class with no deterministic pattern: it lives in the standard's review checklist and in your AI agent's [rules file](../../integrations), so the model reviews for it.

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the two accounts that must differ but are never checked with require_keys_neq! (non-Anchor-Account paths).
