# SOL-009 · CPI without authority check

`invoke_signed` without verifying the caller's authority (signing with a PDA ≠ authorization). Fix: check authority before the CPI.

**Enforcement — machine-checkable.** A deterministic pattern flags this automatically in the [CLI](../../cli), the [GitHub Action](../../action.yml), the [Semgrep ruleset](../../semgrep), the [VS Code extension](../../extensions/vscode), and the [MCP server](../../mcp).

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the invoke_signed call and the missing upstream caller-authority check.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. the caller's authority for THIS SPECIFIC privileged operation is verified (Signer + has_one/constraint binding the caller to the target resource) BEFORE the invoke_signed — a 'trusted target program' or any unrelated upstream Signer check is NOT sufficient
