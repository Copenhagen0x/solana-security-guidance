# SOL-040 · Credit from requested, not measured (Token-2022)

A deposit/vault handler credits shares from the REQUESTED `amount` instead of the MEASURED pre/post delta of the destination token account. Under a Token-2022 mint with a transfer-fee or transfer-hook the vault receives strictly less than `amount`, so the depositor is over-credited and the vault under-collateralized; the surplus drains to the next withdrawer. Distinct from SOL-032 (decimals) and SOL-013 (wrong token program). Fix: credit the measured pre/post balance delta (`reload()` before/after), not the requested amount; pin BOTH the destination ATA mint AND authority (a measured delta alone is not enough)

**Enforcement — review-only.** A judgment-call class with no deterministic pattern: it lives in the standard's review checklist and in your AI agent's [rules file](../../integrations), so the model reviews for it.

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the deposit/mint-shares handler that credits an internal balance/share field from the requested transfer `amount` (or an instruction-data amount field) rather than from a measured pre/post balance delta of the program's destination token account — AND/OR a deposit handler whose destination token account or accounting mint is not pinned to the vault's program-controlled authority, so an honest delta measures an attacker-substituted account or a worthless attacker-minted asset.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. the credited amount is computed from a measured pre/post delta — the destination token account is reload()ed and its `.amount` read both immediately BEFORE and immediately AFTER the transfer CPI, and the credit is `post.checked_sub(pre)` (not the `amount` argument); a single post-transfer read without a captured pre-balance is NOT sufficient when the account can hold a prior balance
2. BINDING (mandatory in addition to the measured delta — a measured delta alone does NOT clear this rule): the destination token account is pinned to a program-controlled authority and the mint is pinned/validated against the vault's accounting asset, with both constraints VISIBLE IN THE ACCOUNTS STRUCT — e.g. `#[account(mut, associated_token::mint = mint, associated_token::authority = vault)]` (or seeds-derived `token::authority = <vault pda>` + `token::mint = mint`) on the destination account, plus `has_one = mint` on the vault (or a pinned mint address); if the destination `vault_ata` is a bare `InterfaceAccount<'info, TokenAccount>` with no authority/mint binding, or the `mint` is unconstrained, the handler is STILL drainable via wrong-account substitution or worthless-mint deposit even when the delta math is correct — do NOT clear it on the strength of the delta
3. the deposit mint is pinned by address to a plain SPL Token mint (token program == anchor_spl::token::ID, not token_2022::ID) OR the handler asserts the Token-2022 mint carries NO TransferFee AND NO TransferHook extension (e.g. via get_extension::<TransferFeeConfig>()/<TransferHook>() returning none — checking extension PRESENCE, fixed at init, not the current fee rate) before crediting — so the received amount provably equals the requested amount; 'we only support standard tokens' without a pin or an extension check is NOT sufficient. (This clears the under-crediting threat only; the BINDING exclusion above still applies independently.)
4. the value moved is a fixed, program-controlled internal transfer where the destination token account's authority is a program-owned PDA visible in the accounts struct AND the mint is pinned, so no external mint with fee/hook semantics can be substituted and no foreign token account can be passed — a positive, in-struct 'no fee path and no substitution can apply here' proof, not a prose 'this looks internal' assertion
5. the `amount` is used only to bound/validate the transfer (e.g. a max-in cap) and is never the source of the credited share/balance figure — the credit is sourced from the measured delta — AND the binding exclusion above is independently satisfied
