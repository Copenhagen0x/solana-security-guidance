# SOL-015 · Anchor constraints missing

`Account<'info, T>` cross-references another account with no `has_one =` / `constraint =`. Fix: tie related accounts together with constraints.

**Enforcement — review-only.** A judgment-call class with no deterministic pattern: it lives in the standard's review checklist and in your AI agent's [rules file](../../integrations), so the model reviews for it.

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the Account<'info, T> that cross-references another account with no has_one / constraint binding.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. the related accounts are bound by has_one / constraint / seeds in the accounts struct
2. the relationship is enforced by an explicit require_keys_eq! in the handler body before use
