# SOL-012 · Rent exemption check missing

Funded account not verified rent-exempt → runtime purges it, state lost. Fix: assert rent-exempt.

**Enforcement — review-only.** A judgment-call class with no deterministic pattern: it lives in the standard's review checklist and in your AI agent's [rules file](../../integrations), so the model reviews for it.

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — medium.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the funded account used without a rent-exempt assertion.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. the account is created with Anchor `init` (rent-exemption enforced by the framework)
2. a rent-exempt assertion (Rent::get) is present before/at funding
