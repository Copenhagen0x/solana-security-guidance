# SOL-007 · Missing owner verification

`AccountInfo` deserialized without `owner == program_id` → attacker passes a same-layout account from another program. Fix: check owner first.

**Enforcement — machine-checkable.** A deterministic pattern flags this automatically in the [CLI](../../cli), the [GitHub Action](../../action.yml), the [Semgrep ruleset](../../semgrep), the [VS Code extension](../../extensions/vscode), and the [MCP server](../../mcp).

**Value tier — HIGH.** HIGH — high-value; surfaced even at a strict submission floor

**Baseline severity — high.** A starting point, not a verdict: calibrate by preconditions × access level (take the lower) per [SEVERITY.md](../../SEVERITY.md).

**Reachability anchor.** To confirm this is exploitable (not merely present), cite the AccountInfo deserialize whose owner is never compared to program_id.

**Do NOT flag when** — cite the number when suppressing a finding:

1. account.owner == program_id (or the expected program) is checked before deserializing
2. the account is an Anchor `Account<'info, T>` (Anchor enforces the owner) rather than a raw AccountInfo
3. the bytes are read only for display/logging and are never persisted, emitted in an event, or trusted for any authorization or fund decision — on-chain OR by an off-chain consumer of the emitted data
